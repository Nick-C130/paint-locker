How to get a good reading
  • Paint a swatch on card (or use a painted model) and put a grey card or plain white paper right next to it, in the same light.
  • Use daylight from a window or a daylight lamp. Avoid direct sun, mixed lighting and glare.
  • Fill the frame, keep the phone parallel to the card, and don't use flash on gloss paint.
  • The card fixes the colour cast of the light and camera. Without it, readings can be off by a lot.
