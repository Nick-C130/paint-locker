📥 Import your collection

Bring in the paints you own from a spreadsheet, another paint app's export, or a plain list. Nothing is added until you've checked the preview.

Upload a file

CSV, TSV or Excel (.xlsx; the first sheet). Columns are recognised by name: brand, range/line, code/SKU, name, barcode/EAN, quantity, owned (yes/no), rack/location, cell/slot, date bought, notes. You can fix the mapping on the next page.

Works with Paint Locker's own inventory export, and with exports from other paint apps and trackers that save a CSV or spreadsheet. If an app marks paints as owned or wishlist, rows marked not owned are skipped.

Or paste a list

One paint per line: a code, a name, or both, with an optional brand. “2x …” or “… x3” sets how many bottles.

Adding paints to the catalogue instead (new brands or lines)? Use catalogue CSV import.
